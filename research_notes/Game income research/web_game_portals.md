# Web/HTML5 game portals: what small devs actually earn

Researched 2026-10-08. Note on method: most primary pages (portal docs, Reddit, Medium, Substack) were blocked to direct fetch, so many details come from search-engine extracts of those pages. **[V]** = from the platform's own docs or a named developer's own dated post. **[C]** = a claim (self-reported with no proof, vendor marketing, or a secondhand compilation). Check every term in the portal dashboard before you rely on it.

## 2. Concrete earnings data (the scarce part)

Portals don't publish per-game revenue. The data points I found:

- **CrazyGames, ~2018 (DonislawDev):** 8 WebGL games, 451,327 plays, **€556.92 total**, which is about **€1.20 per 1k plays** [V, old] ([donislawdev.com](https://donislawdev.com/earnings-and-statistics-from-my-8-games-android-ios-webgl/)).
- **Anul Agarwal (solo, AI-assisted):** one CrazyGames game had **50k plays ≈ $250, about $5 per 1k plays** [C, self-reported]. He built a Suika/watermelon clone with ChatGPT in about 2 weeks (2023). He first reported **~$10k** from web portals plus organic mobile ([dev.to](https://dev.to/anulagarwal12/i-built-a-game-using-chatgpt-in-2-weeks-that-made-10000-1n2i)). He later reported **$25k+, 60M+ players**, mainly on Coolmath ([Medium](https://medium.com/@anulagarwal12/my-ai-generated-game-earned-25-000-here-is-how-it-happened-ea5612d91464)). He says his **first ~100 of 120+ prototypes "barely made any money"** and the next ~20 made "close to six figures" in total ([X article via YouMind, Oct 2026](https://youmind.com/landing/x-viral-articles/ai-web-games-passive-income)). Caveat: he now sells a Claude Code "AI games kit", so he has an incentive to make the results look good.
- **CrazyGames dev (Liquid Swarm):** about **€31/day** six weeks after Full Launch. A rewarded ad lifted revenue ~25% [C, via compilation] ([GitHub report](https://github.com/pocketagent98-ai/html5-game-opportunity-report)).
- **Poki successes give plays, not dollars:** Artem Lanin had **67M plays in 2025** across 5 games, and web became his primary income ([LinkedIn](https://ua.linkedin.com/in/artemlanin)). Blumgi had 100M+ plays in ~2 yrs and lives "comfortably". Poki says top studios earn up to ~$1M/yr ([TFN](https://techfundingnews.com/browser-gaming-website-poki-won-big-at-the-dutch-game-awards-celebrating-hitting-1-billion-monthly-plays/)) [C].
- **Low end:** a forum reply put typical games at "$1–$2 per game" per month ([html5gamedevs](https://www.html5gamedevs.com/topic/40362-monetising-html5-games/)). A new GameDistribution dev earned **€0.19** in their first week ([ModDB](https://www.moddb.com/news/my-first-week-earnings-from-gamedistribution-a-small-step-towards-big-goals)). GameDistribution's own blog claims an "average" dev makes "a couple thousand euros/month" ([GD blog](https://blog.gamedistribution.com/html5-indie-game-developer-opportunities-2026/)), which is marketing and contradicted by the forum reports [C].
- **eCPM benchmarks (vendor):** rewarded video $15–28 US, $1–3 tier-3. Realized web-network average $3.62 globally, $6.98 US ([AppLixir](https://www.applixir.com/blog/ecpm-optimization-for-web-games-benchmarks-and-fixes/)) [C].

**Synthesis:** a realistic developer take is **~$0.5–5 per 1,000 plays** after the portal's share, depending on geography and ad placement. A game needs **roughly 50k–400k plays/month to earn $200/month**. The distribution is a power law: the median game earns cents to single-digit dollars a month, a decent game earns ~$20–300, and hits earn $1k+. I found no verified median.

## 3. AI mass-production cases and pushback

- **Pieter Levels, fly.pieter.com (Feb 2025):** a browser flight sim built with Cursor/LLMs in hours. He claimed **$1M ARR / $87k MRR in 17 days**, almost entirely from **direct ad sponsorships**, not portals. That income came from his ~600k X followers. 404 Media found only ~$360 came from in-game purchases. The revenue is self-reported, other outlets report conflicting figures, and one secondhand report says it later collapsed ([404 Media](https://404media.co/this-game-created-by-ai-vibe-coding-makes-50-000-a-month-yours-probably-wont)). This doesn't transfer to an owner with no audience.
- **Anul Agarwal** (above) is the closest analog to this plan: LLM-built clones of trending mechanics, released in volume to portals. Even in his telling, the hit rate was about 1 in 5–6 among later games, after ~100 early misses.
- **Pushback:** Poki's AI page explicitly targets template or AI-heavy games lacking originality, and it may decline crowded genres. CrazyGames rejects clones and asset flips and **restricts repeat low-quality submitters**. itch.io requires disclosure. Newgrounds is anti-AI. Steam had 7,300+ AI-disclosed games by March 2026, and "gameslop" backlash is growing ([TechBuzz](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/ai-in-game-development-why-the-2026-backlash-is-real)). I found no documented case of a portal banning a developer specifically for AI, but the stated rules make **volume spam of near-identical clones the main ban and rejection risk**.

## 4. Timelines (estimated)

- **CrazyGames:** QA (days to 2 wks), then Basic Launch (2 wks), then Full Launch revisions. Paid monthly once €100 accrues. **First payout is plausibly 2–4 months** after first submission, and only if a game passes Basic Launch.
- **Poki:** tests take about 1 week, final review 1–2 wks, then integration. **First payout is plausibly 2–4 months** after acceptance. Access is gated and the portal is in closed beta.
- **GameDistribution:** immediate listing, but a 60-day payment lag on top of a €100 minimum. At the low-end rates above, a small game could take a year or more to reach the minimum.

## 5. Bottom line for this owner

- **Best fit:** **CrazyGames first** (open, no AI ban, Tipalti/ACH, US-friendly, the Basic Launch gives free data on whether a game works). Pitch **Poki** only with a game that clearly passes CrazyGames metrics, because exclusivity would lock it off CrazyGames. Use itch.io only as a disclosed-AI portfolio and showcase. Skip Newgrounds, Y8, Telegram, Facebook Instant Games and Discord. Treat YouTube Playables as opportunistic only (closed pilot).
- **Strategy that respects the rules:** 1–2 *original-twist*, polished games per month, not clone volume. Claude Code does the build, QA and SDK integration. The owner's 2 hrs/week go to playtesting for "fun", art direction and sign-off, uploads, and replying to QA feedback. Keep a prompt and iteration log, since Poki can request one. Disclose AI where required. Cash cost is ~$0 apart from optional asset packs (<$100).
- **Realistic net $/month** (no external audience, median-to-decent outcomes):
  - **Month 3:** **$0–20.** Likely zero cash received yet, because of Basic Launch gates and the €100 minimum.
  - **Month 6:** **$10–80** from 4–8 games live, with most games earning under $5/mo and maybe one earning $20–60.
  - **Month 12:** **$30–250**, median guess **~$75**. Reaching $200+/mo needs one breakout (~200k+ plays/month), and the evidence suggests roughly a 10–20% chance per year at this effort level.
- **Owner hours:** about 2 hrs/week is enough *only if* Claude handles everything else. Expect 4–6 hrs in month 1 for accounts, Tipalti/W-9 tax info and the first submissions.
- **Verdict:** web portals can probably cover *part* of the $200 target within 12 months, but I'd treat **$200/month net as unlikely (<25%) in year 1** from portals alone. They work best as a low-cost add-on to a stronger income channel, not as the main plan.
