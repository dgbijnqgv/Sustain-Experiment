# Game-related digital products: what solo creators actually earn (AI-heavy production)

Researched 2026-10-08. Method: web search only. Most primary pages (itch.io, Substack, Unity forums, DriveThru help center) were blocked for direct fetch, so many figures below come from search-result excerpts of those pages. **[V]** = self-reported by the creator in a primary post (verified only as "they said it"). **[E]** = third-party estimate/model. **[C]** = unsourced claim (marketing/SEO guide). Treat everything as indicative.

## 1. Code assets, tools, templates, plugins, shaders

**Fees and AI policy**
- **Unity Asset Store:** 70/30 split, payouts monthly via PayPal ([Unity docs](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/asset-store/publishing/asset-packages/revenue/payouts)). AI content is allowed, but **any AI involvement, "including the use of AI in code," must be stated in the marketing data**. Low-value or derivative work is rejected ([Unity support](https://support.unity.com/hc/en-us/articles/16456407029524-Can-I-publish-and-sell-content-generated-with-AI-on-the-Asset-Store)). Listings carry a "Created with AI" tag ([example listing](https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/tools/utilities/ai-context-builder-project-exporter-for-chatgpt-claude-gemini-377656)).
- **Fab (Epic/Unreal):** sellers keep 88% ([fab.com/about](https://fab.com/about)). After one user uploaded about 38,000 AI assets, Epic added a mandatory "Created With AI" declaration, upload limits and a buyer filter to hide AI items ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/epic-games-adresses-ai-generated-content-plaguing-fab)).
- **itch.io:** the creator sets the platform cut, with a default of 10%. Since Nov 2024, **asset pages must answer a generative-AI disclosure question, including a "Code" option**. Untagged AI assets are de-indexed or delisted ([GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2024/11/itchio-store-now-requires-ai-generated-content-disclosures-for-assets), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/asset-creators-on-itch-io-now-have-to-disclose-the-use-of-generative-ai/)).
- **Godot:** the official Asset Store launched in beta around May 2026. **Paid listings are limited to selected creators first**, and no public fee schedule was found ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/game-engine-godot-rolls-out-asset-store-in-early-beta), [Oflight analysis](https://www.oflight.co.jp/en/columns/godot-asset-store-launch-2026)). Paid Godot add-ons are sold mainly on itch.io today.
- **GameDev Market:** **became fully generative-AI-free.** From 27 Aug 2025 sellers had four weeks to remove gen-AI assets, and new AI submissions are rejected ([GDM announcement](https://gamedevmarket.net/news/an-important-update-on-generative-ai-assets-on-gdm)). Treat it as closed to an AI-built catalog.

**Earnings evidence**
- Unity, old but primary: one publisher reported about $1–2k/month net, with $5–9k in a featured-sale month. Another, ranked around 250th, averaged about $1k/month. A small plugin publisher fell to **$30–60/month** ([Unity Discussions](https://discussions.unity.com/t/how-much-asset-store-publishers-earn/628535)) [V, around 2015–16]. One debut asset made **$56 in month 1 and about $317/month by month 6** ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/what-is-the-unity-asset-store-and-how-to-make-money-on-it-)) [V, around 2018].
- Unity, 2025–26 trend: commentators say "random hobbyist sales are fading as AI covers cheap code." Premium editor tools and complete projects with support still sell ([Darko Unity](https://darkounity.com/blog/is-the-unity-asset-store-dying), [Makaka](https://makaka.org/unity-tutorials/asset-store-publisher)). One 2026 guide gives tiers of "hobbyist $50–200/mo, mid $1–5k/mo" ([generalistprogrammer](https://generalistprogrammer.com/tutorials/unity-asset-store-selling-guide-revenue)) [C].
- itch.io assets: one creator released 6 asset packs plus zines in 2025, with **$1,338 in art-market sales** inside $8,850 of total revenue, mostly freelance work ([itch.io blog "2025 Finances"](https://itch.io/blog/1137874/2025-finances)) [V]. Another made **$378.99 from itch** out of $5,454 total revenue in 2024 ([itch.io blog "2024 Finances"](https://itch.io/blog/851643/2024-finances)) [V]. A forum seller makes "$20–30/month after 30+ hours/month" of work. One YouTuber reported "basically zero sales" in their first year of pixel packs ([itch forum](https://itch.io/t/796798/how-much-your-work-worth-), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gzOHOPT1ApQ)) [V].
- Godot plugins: no hard data was found. Figures like "$300–2,000/month per popular plugin" are illustrative only ([Caminho Solo](https://caminhosolo.com.br/en/2026/03/godot-engine-solopreneurs-earn-money/)) [C].
- **Case of an LLM-built code asset with published sales: none found.** Several AI-tagged Unity tools exist, but none disclose revenue.

**Takeaway:** code is the category best suited to AI production. The typical new tool still earns **$0–100/month in its first 6 months**. Reaching $200+/month seems to require a niche, well-documented tool with support, priced around $15–40, ideally on Unity or Fab where buyers pay. Every listing must disclose AI use.

## 2. Small paid games (itch.io, Steam)

- **Steam fee:** $100 per app (Steam Direct), recoupable once the game passes $1,000 gross. Valve keeps 30%.
- **Steam AI disclosure:** since Jan 2024, pre-generated and live-generated AI content must be disclosed in the Content Survey ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey)). A **Jan 2026 revision** says "efficiency gains through the use of these tools is not the focus." Disclosure now targets AI content "that ships with your game, and is consumed by players," including store page and marketing ([Blues News](https://www.bluesnews.com/s/293484/steam-ai-disclosure-changes), [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-updates-AI-disclosure-form-requiring-developers-to-report-visible-and-in-game-AI-but-not-background-tools.1206103.0.html)). AI-written code alone likely needs no store label, but AI art, text or audio does. Confirm against the live Steamworks page.
- **Median revenue:** VG Insights/Alinea data puts the **2025 median Steam game at $249 gross**, with 66% under $1k ([via Ziva](https://ziva.sh/blogs/indie-game-revenue)) [E]. Gamalytic found **50% of 2020–23 releases earned at most $500**. Paid games over $5 had a median of about $4k, and over $10 about $17k ([GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gamalytic-67-of-games-on-steam-earned), [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-42136)) [E].
- **Wishlist economics:** the median first-week sales ratio is about 0.10–0.15× launch wishlists ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/steam-the-new-wishlists-to-first)). 66% of games launch with fewer than 10k wishlists ([VGI via GameDevReports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/video-game-insights-steam-wishlists)).
- **A hit for contrast:** the solo-made *Only Way Is Down* ($9.99) launched with 17,821 wishlists. It sold 8,656 copies, about **$41k net**, after about a year of development ([postmortem](https://onlywayisdown.com/post-mortem/)) [V]. That is well beyond an owner with 2 hours/week.
- **itch.io is weak for paid games:** one game sold about 113k copies on Steam but only **414 sales ($707) on itch** ([Skydome devlog](https://skydome.itch.io/thehive/devlog/1142956/itchio-sales-reality-check-and-why-it-doesnt-mean-your-game-is-bad)) [V].
- **LLM-built games:** *Meet Claude* (Steam, 2026) is free and fully AI-disclosed. A Claude Code game won a **$25k Vibe Jam prize, not sales** ([MacObserver](https://www.macobserver.com/news/ios-developer-wins-25000-after-building-entire-game-with-claude-code-in-two-weeks/)). No vibe-coded Steam game with published sales was found.

**Takeaway:** the median outcome is under $500 lifetime. Steam is a lottery that needs marketing time the owner doesn't have. Steam does accept AI-coded games, and the code alone doesn't trigger a label.

## 3. Tabletop, print-and-play and solo journaling RPGs

- **DriveThruRPG / OneBookShelf:** royalty is **65% non-exclusive, 70% exclusive** ([DTRPG partners](https://help.drivethrupartners.com/hc/en-us/articles/12780816206871-Exclusive-vs-Non-Exclusive-Partner-Accounts)). The current Product Standards say **"We do not accept any product that includes text generated by AI tools."** Standalone AI art and AI token/map packs are banned. AI art inside books requires the "Contains AI-Generated Content" filter ([Product Standards](https://help.drivethrupartners.com/hc/en-us/articles/12780748778135-Product-Standards-Guidelines), [EN World](https://www.enworld.org/threads/dms-guild-and-drivethrurpg-ban-ai-written-works-requires-labels-for-ai-generated-art.698936/)). **An AI-written RPG is effectively banned on DTRPG.**
- **Community climate:** the ENnies refuse any gen-AI product from the 2025–26 cycle. Chaosium's community programs ban AI art ([search summary of ENnies/Chaosium](https://www.chaosium.com/blogai-art-and-chaosium-community-content-programs-miskatonic-repositoryjonstown-compendiumexplorers-society/)). The indie TTRPG audience on itch.io is strongly anti-AI, so AI tags hurt discovery.
- **Earnings:** a zine creator grossed **about $2,400 in 2025** across their catalog, 43.8% from itch, and called DTRPG "worth it, but barely" ([Substack](https://monstermind.substack.com/p/2025-a-year-in-review)) [V]. Another earned **$288.66 on DTRPG for all of 2024** ([Buttondown](https://buttondown.com/Binary/archive/2024-year-in-review-by-numbers/)) [V]. A layout freelancer made $62.78 from marketplaces in 2024 ([itch blog](https://itch.io/blog/851643/2024-finances)) [V]. Hits exist (*Journey* reached DTRPG Platinum) but are human-designed ([Graycastle](https://graycastlepress.itch.io/journey/devlog/365932/uhh-journey-just-went-platinum-yall)).

**Takeaway:** the policy and audience risk is high and typical revenue is tens to hundreds of dollars a year. Viable only if the owner writes the game and AI handles layout, PDFs and tooling.

## 4. GDD templates and Notion/Figma kits (Gumroad, itch.io)

- **Gumroad:** **10% + $0.50** on direct sales, **30%** on Discover-marketplace sales. Gumroad has been merchant of record since 1 Jan 2025 and handles sales tax ([Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing), [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees)). Gumroad has no AI ban. itch requires the disclosure only on asset pages.
- **Market:** existing GDD templates sell at $5–10 or pay-what-you-want ([OccaSoftware](https://occasoftware.gumroad.com/l/game-design-document-template), [vitalzigns](https://vitalzigns.gumroad.com/l/GDD)), and free GDD templates are common ([Wayline](https://wayline.io/resources/game-design-document-template)). An analysis of about 146k Gumroad products found a **median of about 28 lifetime sales, about $364**, with 0.5% passing 1,000 sales ([InsightRaider](https://insightraider.com/fr/answers/how-to-sell-templates-online)) [E]. Notion "I made $60k" stories are self-promotional [C].

**Takeaway:** easy to make with AI and has no AI ban. It is commoditized, so expect $0–50/month without an audience. It works better as a lead magnet or bundle add-on for bigtarogames.com.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Time to first sale:** a few weeks for marketplace assets (Unity example: $56 in month 1). It can be never on itch.io without promotion. Steam requires months of wishlist building.
2. **Disclosure is now universal** (Unity, Fab, itch.io assets, Steam player-facing content, DTRPG). **Outright bans** apply at GameDev Market (all gen-AI) and DTRPG (AI text, AI art packs). Honest AI tags are mandatory and reduce visibility with art and TTRPG buyers. Code-tool buyers mind less.
3. **No documented case** found of an LLM-built game asset, game or TTRPG earning a published, steady $200/month. Vibe-coding income stories (e.g., Forbes "$2k/month") are SaaS/web, not game marketplaces ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jodiecook/2026/04/15/how-to-vibe-code-2000month-in-extra-income-this-week/)).

## Bottom line for this owner

Ranked by expected **net $/month** (median-ish case, with optimistic case in brackets), assuming AI does nearly all the production and the owner spends about 2 hours/week.

| Rank | Option | Month 3 | Month 6 | Month 12 | Owner hrs/wk | Cash cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Code tools/plugins on Unity Asset Store + Fab + itch.io** (niche editor tools, Godot add-ons on itch), AI disclosed, 3–6 products | $0–30 [$100] | $20–100 [$300] | $50–200 [$500+] | 1.5–2 (review, screenshots, submit, support triage) | about $0 (Unity/Fab free to list) |
| 2 | **GDD/Notion/design templates** on Gumroad + itch.io, bundled with #1 and the site | $0–10 | $5–30 | $10–60 [$150] | 0.5 | $0 |
| 3 | **Small paid game** on itch.io, then Steam once wishlists are above about 2k | $0 | $0–20 | $0–80 lifetime-ish, median under $500 total [rare $1k+/mo] | 2+ and marketing-hungry | $100 Steam fee (within budget) |
| 4 | **Tabletop/solo RPG** (itch.io only; DTRPG bans AI text) | $0–10 | $0–20 | $0–30 | 2+ (must author the text) | $0 |
| — | GameDev Market | Not allowed (gen-AI ban) | | | | |

**Verdict:** none of these reliably reaches $200/month net within 6 months. Code tools are the only option where AI's strength lines up with what buyers pay for and what the platform rules allow. A catalog of about 5–8 focused, well-documented tools across Unity, Fab and itch.io has a plausible but unproven path to $200/month by month 12. Templates are cheap add-ons. Games and TTRPGs are high-variance with a median near zero. Plan for game-marketplace income as a slow supplement, and look elsewhere (e.g., services or non-game SaaS) for the main $200/month.
