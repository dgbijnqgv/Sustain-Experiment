# UGC platforms, cash jams/contests, gig work: what small creators actually earn

Research date: 2026-10-08. Note: the research proxy blocked most primary pages (roblox.com, fortnite.com, reddithelp, meta.com). Most numbers below come from search-indexed excerpts of primary pages or trade press. Tags: **[V]** = primary source or several reputable outlets agree; **[C]** = single secondary or self-reported claim.

## 1. UGC platforms with built-in audiences

### Roblox
- **DevEx rate:** $0.0038 per earned Robux since 5 Sep 2025, up from $0.0035 **[V]**. To cash out you need 30,000 earned Robux (about $114), age 13+, a verified email and an account in good standing ([Roblox Help](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/articles/203314100)) **[V]**. One calculator site claims a $0.0054 tier from June 2026 for spend by age-verified US 18+ users ([agatasmurf](https://agatasmurf.com/devex-calculator/)) **[C, unverified]**.
- **Distribution, RDC 2026 (12 months to 30 Jun 2026):** about $1.7B paid out in total. More than 42,000 creators took part in DevEx. The **median DevEx participant got about $1,500 per year (about $125/month)**. The top 1,000 averaged $1.4M and the top 10 averaged $65.7M ([TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-1-500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/robloxs-top-creators-average-65-7-million-a-year-but-most-make-far-less-3408675/), [SAYS](https://says.com/my/tech/roblox-creators-earn-1-7-billion-developer-payouts)) **[V]**. The prior year's median was $1,440 ([Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-top-developers-average-339-million-in-annual-earnings)). **Caveat:** that median covers only creators who already cleared the $114 cash-out bar. Millions of developers never reach it. One blog's estimate that "~85% earn under $100/mo" is **[C]**.
- **Engagement payouts:** "Creator Rewards" replaced the older payout programs on 25 Jul 2025. The Daily Engagement Reward pays 5 Robux (about $0.02) per qualifying user. A user qualifies only if they spent at least $9.99 in the last 60 days and played your game for at least 10 minutes as one of their first three experiences that day. The Audience Expansion Reward pays 35% of a new or lapsed user's first $100 of spend. Payouts are held for 60 days ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-rewards-is-live/3838257), [Roblox wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Creator_Rewards), [hold-period complaint](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/shorten-the-hold-period-of-engagement-payouts-or-restore-the-old-premium-payouts-system/3956591)) **[V]**. Small games with few paying players earn almost nothing from this.
- **Time to first payout:** reaching 30k Robux, plus the 60-day hold on rewards, puts the first cash at about 3–6+ months even for a game that gets traction. Most never get there.
- **How much AI can do:** a lot of the coding. Roblox publishes an official Studio MCP server (open-source on Roblox's GitHub) that lets Claude/Cursor run Luau, insert models and playtest inside an open Studio session ([mcp.so listing](https://mcp.so/servers/studio-rust-mcp-server), [community server](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@boshyxd/robloxstudio-mcp/blob/3a3876a9d2c79303954292f0939bf9cd492837b6/README.md)). Rojo-style file sync lets code live in git. A human still has to run Studio on a desktop (Win/Mac), publish, verify ID for DevEx, handle moderation and age questionnaires, and judge fun. AI-made assets are allowed: Roblox ships its own generative tools. Roblox commissions are often paid in Robux: 500–1,200 Robux for a small job is about $2–5 after DevEx ([DevForum listing](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/scripter-for-hire-updated/625768)) **[C]**.

### Fortnite (UEFN / Island Creator Program)
- **2024 payout brackets (Epic Year in Review, via [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/fortnite-has-58-creators-that-got-paid-over-usd1-million-in-2024-and-7-of-those-made-over-usd10-million/)):** $352M paid to 70,000 creators ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-paid-352-million-to-fortnite-creators-in-2024/)) **[V]**.
  - About 30,000 creators were paid $100–999 per year.
  - About 5,400 were paid $1k–9.9k.
  - 1,728 were paid $10k–99k.
  - 58 were paid more than $1M.

  **Typical paid creator: under $1k per year.** About half of the 70k creators got under the $100 threshold, which works out to nothing. Total UEFN payouts since 2023 reached $1B as of June 2026 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/06/17/epic-games-unreal-editor-for-fortnite-creator-payouts/)).
- **Rules:** creators must be 18+ to receive payouts. There is a $100 minimum, paid about 30 days after month end through Hyperwallet ([Epic docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fortnite/island-creator-and-engagement-program-in-fortnite-creative), [Prima](https://primagames.com/gaming/fortnite-island-creator-program-guide)). Since 9 Jan 2026 islands can sell items in-game. Creators keep 100% of the V-Bucks value until 31 Jan 2027 and 50% after that ([PocketGamer](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/epic-games-rolls-out-in-island-transactions-for-fortnite-creative)) **[V]**.
- **How much AI can do:** Verse code, yes. But UEFN is a Windows-only Unreal editor with heavy GUI and level work, so the human workload is much higher than on Roblox. Epic says it will not police AI-generated content as such; the test is whether the content follows the rules and is properly licensed ([PC Games Insider](https://www.pcgamesinsider.biz/news/75142/epic-isnt-going-to-be-policing-ai-fortnite-thumbnails/)).

### Others
- **Minecraft Marketplace:** only approved Partners can sell. Partners need a registered business, a portfolio and a review ([Minecraft Partner page](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/partner), [Noxcrew FAQ](https://noxcrew.com/help/minecraft-marketplace-community-creators)). Not viable for this owner.
- **Rec Room:** shut down. Creator earnings stopped 18 May 2026 and the final payout was on 1 Jun 2026 ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/rec-room-shutting-down-seattles-3-5b-social-gaming-platform-says-it-cant-make-the-business-work/)) **[V]**.
- **Core (Manticore):** still running, but the audience is tiny and there is no meaningful payout data. Skip it.
- **Meta Horizon Worlds:** no published creator-earnings data. The platform pivoted to mobile and its VR future wobbled in 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_Worlds)). Its competitions are the real money (see section 2).
- **Reddit Devvit (web games inside Reddit, TypeScript, entirely code-driven, so very AI-friendly):** Developer Funds paid out on engagement tiers. In 2025 and H1 2026, tiers ran from $500 at 500 daily qualified engagers to $75k at 1M ([2025 announcement mirror](https://pol1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/Devvit/comments/1jjj4tk/developer_funds_whats_next)) **[V]**. Under the **H2 2026 terms, the first tier rose to 5,000 daily engagers for $4,000**, with recurring payouts above that ([Reddit H2 2026 terms](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/50860336905108-Reddit-Developer-Funds-H2-2026-Terms)) **[V, excerpt only]**. That bar is much harder for a newcomer. Separately, in-app gold pays $0.01 per gold with a $10 minimum. In Nov 2025 the fund paid more than $163k across many apps ([Devvit blog mirror](https://tourism-pro-counties-prototype.trycloudflare.com/docs/blog/2025/12/18/november-payouts)) **[C]**.

| Event | Recurrence | Prizes | AI policy and odds |
|---|---|---|---|

Jun–Jul 2026 with Phaser: $40k ($15k top) ([Phaser](https://phaser.io/news/2026/06/reddit-and-phaser-launch-a-40-000-game-dev-hackathon)) **[V]** | AI coding not banned. About 15–20 paid slots per event. Entry counts not retrieved. |
| **Meta Horizon Creator Competitions** | 5 in 2025, continuing in 2026 | 2025 series: $1M Genre Showdown (30 winners), $1M+$500k Open Source (35 worlds), $1.5M Mobile Innovation. Meta says more than 5,000 creators took part and more than 1,000 worlds were built across the series ([Meta blog](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/announcing-meta-horizon-mobile-innovation-winners/)) **[V]** | Meta pushes its GenAI tools. Prizes per entered world are unusually generous, but entry needs the Desktop Editor, 18+, MHCP membership and an eligible country. The **2026 Game Design competition was document-only**: design doc, journey map, concept pack and production plan via Devpost. That is very AI-plus-taste friendly; watch for a repeat. |

| **js13kGames** (Aug–Sep) | Annual | Mostly swag. 197 entries in 2025 ([dev.to](https://dev.to/js13kgames/js13kgames-2025-winners-announced-20nl)) | Little cash. |
| **Small itch cash jams** | Ongoing | "It's Based" quarterly jam: ≥$500 pool, about $4k paid over 5 jams ([itch](https://itch.io/jam/summer-25)). **Takeaway:** prizes are lumpy. Sponsored platform contests (Meta, Reddit/Devpost) have far better $/entry than itch jams, and they mostly don't penalize AI coding.

## 3. Freelancing (Fiverr / Upwork / Contra)
- **AI rules:** Fiverr allows AI-assisted work with conditions ([Fiverr AI guidelines](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593)) **[V]**:
  - You must disclose AI use when a buyer asks.
  - You must honour "no-AI" requests.
  - Work must be customized for each order. Delivering the same AI output in bulk to several buyers is banned.
  - You must own the rights to what you deliver.

  Upwork recommends being transparent about AI use and agreeing on tools in the contract ([Upwork community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Bulletin-Board/Using-Generative-AI-on-Upwork/m-p/1447852)). The fee has been variable at 0–15% since May 2025. Connects cost $0.15 each and a typical proposal uses about 4–16 of them ([UpCat summary](https://upcat.app/upwork-fees-explained/)) **[C]**.
- **Market:** after ChatGPT launched, postings for automation-prone freelance work fell about 21%, including about 21% for software/web development, while applications per post rose 8.6% (Management Science paper, via [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/generative-ai-reduces-demand-for-some-freelance-jobs-in-writing-coding-and-design-study-says/)) **[V]**. Clients increasingly do with AI themselves what they used to hire for.
- **New seller reality:** I found no hard 2024–26 data. Anecdotes describe new game-dev gigs with zero orders after more than a month, and say art and assets sell better than "I'll make your game" ([itch thread](https://itch.io/t/5650386/unity-how-can-i-get-orders-on-fiverr-for-sure)) **[C]**. Roblox scripting rates claimed by agencies run $15–60/hr ([Flexiple](https://flexiple.com/cost-to-hire/roblox-developer)) **[C]**, but DevForum jobs often pay in Robux at a few dollars each.
- **Fit problem:** the platforms reward fast replies and back-and-forth with clients. The owner would be the bottleneck at about 2 hrs/week, and the clients deal only with the owner's account, not the AI. Feasible for at most 1–2 small fixed-price orders a month.

## 4. Newsletter / YouTube / Patreon (game design)
- I found **no documented case** of someone working about 2 hrs/week who reached $200/month from game-design content. Substack takes 10% plus Stripe fees ([summary](https://bestwriting.com/substack-statistics)). An indie newsletter had 366 subscribers and 1 paid supporter after a year ([example](https://jasonleow.substack.com/p/one-year-of-my-indie-journey-on-substack)) **[C]**.
- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours (not re-verified here). Since 15 Jul 2025 YouTube has tightened enforcement against "mass-produced/repetitious" (often AI) content ([Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/youtube-targets-mass-produced-content-in-monetization-update/550337/)). An AI-heavy channel risks demonetization.

## Bottom line for this owner
These are realistic **net $/month** figures (median-ish expectation, not the best case). Owner hours are per week.

| Rank | Option | 3 mo | 6 mo | 12 mo | Owner hrs/wk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sponsored contests** (Meta Horizon competitions, Reddit/Devpost hackathons, Gamedev.js challenges) | $0 | $0–150 avg | $50–250 avg (lumpy: one $1k–20k win pays for years) | 1–2, in bursts | Best EV per hour. AI does the code and docs; the owner adds taste and the submission video. Watch eligibility (18+, region, membership deadlines). |
| 2 | **Reddit Devvit games** (pairs with #1) | $0 | $0–50 | $0–200 | ~1 | 100% code-based. H2 2026 fund bar is now 5k daily engagers; hackathons are the real money. |
| 3 | **Roblox game** (AI via MCP/Rojo, owner publishes) | $0 | $0–25 | $0–100 | 1–2 | Median DevEx participant earns about $125/mo, and most games never reach the $114 cash-out. Ad tests would need over $100 of budget, which needs owner approval. |
| 4 | **Freelance gigs** (Fiverr/Upwork, HTML5 prototypes, Luau fixes) | $0–50 | $0–150 | $50–300 | 2+, plus fast client replies | The only option that pays reliably per unit of work, but client handling breaks the 2-hr limit. Disclose AI use when asked. |
| 5 | **Fortnite UEFN** | $0 | $0 | $0–80 | 3+ (Windows GUI) | Typical paid creator earns under $1k/yr. Too editor-heavy for this owner. |
| 6 | **Newsletter/YouTube/Patreon** | $0 | $0 | $0–50 | 1–2 | No evidence that 2 hrs/wk reaches $200/mo. |
| — | Minecraft Marketplace, Rec Room, Core | n/a | n/a | n/a | — | Closed to individuals, shut down, or negligible. |

**Verdict:** none of these reliably clears $200/month net within 12 months on 2 hrs/week. The highest-EV mix is a contest pipeline (#1 + #2), with a Roblox game as a long-tail lottery ticket. Freelancing should be used only if the owner accepts more client time.
