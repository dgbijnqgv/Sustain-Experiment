# AI-Generated Content and Commerce Channels: Do They Still Pay with Mostly Automated Operation? (as of 7 Oct 2026)

**How this was researched.** About 28 tool calls (WebSearch in standard and extended modes, plus WebFetch attempts). WebFetch was **blocked** (EGRESS_BLOCKED) for every primary policy source tried: kdp.amazon.com, support.google.com (YouTube), developers.google.com (Google Search Central), etsy.com, help.medium.com, beehiiv.com and musicbusinessworldwide.com. Policy statements below therefore come from **search snippets and secondary reporting**, not the platforms' own pages, unless marked otherwise. The search tool returned synthesized summaries of snippets, so every figure should be read as "seen in snippet, not full text". Every figure and policy carries a date.

**Evidence labels used throughout**
- **[VERIFIED]**: court records, platform newsrooms or primary data, or a reputable outlet reporting such data.
- **[PLATFORM-MARKETING]**: a platform's own case studies or projections. These are self-interested.
- **[SELF-REPORTED/GURU]**: creator or vendor claims. Many come from sellers of courses or tools, which is course-seller bias.
- **[UNCONFIRMED]**: repeated across blogs with no primary source found.

**Who this is judged for.** An autonomous Claude Code agent. The owner does a one-time account setup and refuses spam, deception and ToS violations.

---

## Key Question 1: Which (if any) channels produce ≥$200/month with mostly automated operation in 2026, with verifiable evidence?

### Takeaway
I found **no channel with verifiable, independent evidence** that a mostly-automated, AI-generated operation reliably clears $200/month in 2026 while staying compliant.

- The large, verified AI-content revenue figures come from two sources:
  - operations that platforms later shut down or demonetized, such as YouTube "AI slop" channels terminated in January 2026;
  - outright fraud, such as Michael Smith's $8M streaming scheme, which ended in an 18-month prison sentence on 6 Oct 2026.
- The remaining positive evidence is weak. It is either platform marketing (beehiiv case studies, which involve human operators) or guru/vendor claims. The one data-backed per-asset figure (Adobe Stock AI images at about $0.17 per image per month) implies you need more than 1,000 accepted AI images just to reach roughly $170/month, and that figure predates heavy saturation.
- **Least-bad candidates** for a compliant agent are Adobe Stock (it explicitly accepts disclosed AI content) and a disclosed, genuinely useful AI-assisted niche newsletter on beehiiv. Neither has verified evidence of ≥$200/month under automation.

### Cited Findings

#### Amazon KDP (books)
**Policy: disclosure.** KDP requires authors to disclose *AI-generated* text, images or translations, even if they edited them afterward. *AI-assisted* work (brainstorming, editing, proofreading of human-written content) does not need disclosure. The disclosure is internal and is not shown to shoppers. Sources: [publishing.co.uk guide](https://publishing.co.uk/guides/kdp-ai-content-disclosure/); [NPR, 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/03/13/1237888126/growing-number-ai-scam-books-amazon); [Boston 25, Jan 2026](https://www.boston25news.com/news/local/25-investigates-authors-warn-ai-generated-copycat-books-sold-amazon/KYBOEOY5YBBPBPEJXYMCSH2JJE/).

**Policy: publishing limits.** The original "3 titles per day" cap was introduced in Sept 2023 alongside the AI disclosure rule ([Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazon-adds-to-kdp-generative-ai-policy-caps-daily-self-publishing-uploads/)). The limits have changed since:
- In 2025 it became **10 titles per format per week**.
- **From 21 Sept 2026** it is **2 new titles per format per week** (eBook, paperback and hardcover each), resetting Sunday 00:00 UTC.
- The cap applies only to creating new titles. Existing and already-drafted titles are not affected.
- Sources: [ALLi, "Amazon cuts KDP uploads to two titles a week"](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/podcast-amazon-cuts-kdp-uploads-to-two-titles-a-week/); [Vappingo](https://www.vappingo.com/word-blog/kdp-publishing-limit/); [publishflow.ai](https://publishflow.ai/knowledge-hub/blog/kdp-title-creation-limit/).
- Scope is disputed. One summary says Publishers Lunch described it as 2 per week across all formats, while Amazon's help page reportedly says per format ([search summary of KDP sources](https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/amazon-kdp-ai-generated-content-surge-enforcement-2026)). Amazon's own page could not be fetched.

**Enforcement (2026), [UNCONFIRMED]: blog-reported only.**
- The reported escalation runs: request to update disclosure → title suppression → removal → account flag → suspension.
- An "April 2026 sweep" of titles flagged by AI detection is reported but has no Amazon primary source.
- Source: [AI Policy Desk](https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/amazon-kdp-ai-generated-content-surge-enforcement-2026).
- Monthly new e-book releases reportedly nearly tripled from 2022 to 2025 ([same source](https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/amazon-kdp-ai-generated-content-surge-enforcement-2026)). No primary data was found for this either.

**Reputational and legal context.** AI knock-offs and impersonation books are a recurring press story:
- [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/amazon-ai-book-knockoffs-1235450690/): Talia Lavin found at least 5 AI rip-offs of her work.
- [NBC News video, 3 Oct 2026](https://nbcnews.com/video/how-ai-is-impersonating-authors-work-270953541767): "parasite authors".
- [Boston 25, Jan 2026](https://www.boston25news.com/news/local/25-investigates-authors-warn-ai-generated-copycat-books-sold-amazon/KYBOEOY5YBBPBPEJXYMCSH2JJE/).
- [The Inkplots](https://www.theinkplots.com/p/the-scam-books-showing-up-days-after): Jane Friedman found 6 books falsely bearing her name.

**Earnings evidence.**
- I found **no verified data** on earnings of AI-assisted publishers.
- One 2026 vendor guide claims low-content books earn $2–$5 royalty per sale and "most beginners earn $50–$300/month within 6 months with 10–20 books" ([theeliteengines](https://theeliteengines.com/guides/kdp-low-content-books-money.html)). **[SELF-REPORTED/GURU]**
- A 2025 Substack author reports ~1,000 books created, ~260 live and ~10 selling daily ([Royalties on Demand](https://dannyondemand.substack.com/p/shocking-lies-about-amazon-kdp)). This is an anecdote from a seller of KDP coaching.

**Royalty mechanics.**
- Paperbacks at or below $9.98 reportedly pay 50% royalty, down from 60% since 2025, less print cost ([hjsmithwilliams](https://hjsmithwilliams.substack.com/p/fictional-publishing-news-week-of)).
- Kindle Unlimited reportedly pays about $0.0044 per page read (June 2025) ([same](https://hjsmithwilliams.substack.com/p/fictional-publishing-news-week-of)).
- The 70% eBook band ceiling is disputed: $9.99 vs $12.99 "since 7 July 2026" ([kdpeasy](https://www.kdpeasy.com/guides/2026-kdp-royalty-rates); [inkfluenceai](https://www.inkfluenceai.com/make-money-selling-ai-ebooks)).

#### Etsy and print-on-demand
**Etsy's Creativity Standards (2024 onward).**
- Every listing carries one of four labels: "Made by" / "Designed by" / "Sourced by" / "Handpicked".
- Seller-prompted AI art belongs under **"Designed by"**, not "Made by".
- Selling AI prompt bundles on their own is reportedly not allowed.
- Sources: [Printify help: Etsy Creativity Standards](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4578896595345-What-should-I-know-about-Etsy-s-Creative-Standards-policy); [TechCrunch, 9 Jul 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/etsy-new-seller-policy-2024-generative-ai); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards).

**10 June 2025 revision.**
- Digital downloads were explicitly brought into the Creativity Standards.
- Reselling third-party templates as your own is prohibited.
- Items cut or printed from someone else's template no longer count as "Made by".
- Scanned-vintage downloads were dropped from "Handpicked".
- Sources: [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards); [ngini](https://ngini.com/en-us/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-policy-2026-explained). The etsy.com policy page was blocked.

**Enforcement.**
- AI-disclosure enforcement reportedly began on **14 Jan 2026**.
- In **Q1 2026**, about 12,000 listings were reportedly removed and about 8,500 warnings issued. **[UNCONFIRMED]**: these numbers trace to one unnamed source and no Etsy release was found ([Lineart.ink](https://lineart.ink/blog/etsy-ai-compliance-guide); [iscompliant](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)).
- Appeals are reportedly possible only for listings removed after 15 Jul 2025.

**Saturation.**
- [Digital Trends, Sept 2026](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/) reports sellers quitting as "AI slop floods" Etsy:
  - a UK illustrator says his sales fell 98% in a year;
  - a knitwear designer says her pattern sales fell at least 50% in 6 months (via CBC).
- In Q3 2025, active buyers were down 5% to 86.6M and active sellers down 10.9% to 5.5M ([Penluma analysis](https://penluma.com/blog/make-money-with-ai/16-print-on-demand-etsy/)). These are secondary figures, not checked against Etsy's 10-Q.
- Guides describe planners, wall art and printables as "racing to zero" ([listifyai](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/why-etsy-digital-downloads-not-selling)).

**Merch by Amazon and Redbubble.**
- No official AI-design policy pages were found.
- Third-party blogs disagree on whether Merch requires AI disclosure ([merchbanao](https://merchbanao.com/blog/amazon-merch-ai-policy/); [removeailabel](https://removeailabel.com/blog/merch-by-amazon-ai-generated-designs-policy)).
- Redbubble has no blanket AI ban reported. Enforcement focuses on IP, trademark and spam ([profitlab360](https://profitlab360.com/blog/can-you-sell-ai-art-on-redbubble)).
- Earnings claims such as "top-tier Merch sellers average $3,200/month" are unsourced ([amzprep](https://amzprep.com/amazon-merch-on-demand/)). **[SELF-REPORTED/GURU]**

**Printify.**
- 17 Sept 2026 terms updates cover its AI tools. Users must "review, edit, and approve AI-Generated Content before publishing" ([Printify policy updates](https://printify.com/policies/updates/)). **This is a human-review clause, which matters for a fully autonomous agent.**
- A New York law requiring disclosure of AI-generated humans in ads took effect 9 Jun 2026, per Printify's help article. It is relevant to AI mockups with synthetic models ([Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/47658708553617-Do-I-need-to-disclose-that-my-AI-mockups-show-AI-generated-models)).

**Earnings.** I found no verified, independent Etsy or POD earnings data for AI-driven shops.

#### Stock media
**Adobe Stock: accepts generative AI.**
- The "Created using generative AI tools" checkbox is mandatory.
- AI content is barred from Illustrative Editorial.
- Contributors should submit **at most 3 iterations of similar assets**. Excess similar submissions count as spam and can lead to "account blocking or termination."
- Sources: [Adobe generative AI FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-faq.html); [Adobe GenAI content guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html). Seen as snippets only.
- Adobe introduced new upload limits ([Adobe community announcement](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-37/new-submission-limits-for-adobe-stock-contributors-315961); [CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/)). The numbers were not visible in snippets.
- CineD reports **"nearly half of all images" on Adobe Stock are now AI-generated** ([CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/)).

**Adobe Stock: earnings data. [VERIFIED-ish]** This is analytics-firm data on its own customers, collection date unclear and probably 2023.
- AI images averaged **$0.17 revenue per image per month** vs $0.0375 for non-AI.
- Revenue per download was $1.94 (AI) vs $1.22.
- Only about 9–13% of images ever sold.
- The author expected the AI advantage to shrink as supply grows.
- Source: Stock Performer via [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/AI-generated-images-found-to-make-more-money-on-Adobe-Stock-compared-to-real-counterparts.724535.0.html).
- A widely shared "$3,976 in first year selling AI images on Adobe Stock" comes from a post promoting the author's own keywording tool ([autokeyworder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/ai-stock-photo-earnings-3976)). **[SELF-REPORTED/GURU]**

**Adobe Firefly contributor bonus.**
- The 2026 bonus was paid 17 Sept 2026, covering content used for training between 3 Jun 2025 and 2 Jun 2026.
- Amounts are at Adobe's discretion.
- Minimum cash-out is temporarily $1 (17 Sept–17 Dec 2026), otherwise $25.
- Source: [Adobe community announcements](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-37); [Adobe Firefly contributor FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/firefly-faq.html).

**Shutterstock.**
- Does not accept AI content generated outside its own platform. Its rationale is IP certainty and compensating artists via its Contributor Fund ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/203436/ai-generated-stock-photos-shutterstock-700-million-images); [Vice](https://vice.com/en/article/shutterstock-is-removing-ai-generated-images)).

#### YouTube and TikTok
**YouTube policy.**
- On **15 July 2025** YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to **"inauthentic content"** within YPP monetization policy. TeamYouTube called it "a minor update to our longstanding guideline."
- The targets are mass-produced, templated and repetitive content, such as reading material you didn't create or AI content from generic templates with no original perspective.
- AI use and faceless format are not per se disqualifying.
- Sources: [AlternativeTo, Jul 2025](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content); [ytgrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ai-policy). YouTube's help page was blocked.
- One source claims a July 2026 guidance rewrite named three non-monetizable categories, including AI personas posing as human experts on health, legal, finance or politics ([noobclaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2026/)). **[UNCONFIRMED]**, single source.

**YouTube enforcement.**
- In his January 2026 annual letter, CEO Neal Mohan called managing "AI slop" a top priority for 2026.
- Within days YouTube shut down "more than a dozen" popular AI channels (talking cats, religious figures). A spokesperson cited spam and deceptive-practices rules.
- Sources: [Business Insider via Finviz/Benzinga](https://finviz.com/news/294709/youtube-doesnt-allow-spam-scams-or-other-deceptive-practices-ceo-neal-mohan-says-as-platform-shuts-popular-ai-channels); [Boing Boing, 28 Jan 2026](https://boingboing.net/2026/01/28/slop.html).
- Third-party figures for the wave: 16 channels (11 terminated, 6 wiped), 35M subscribers, 4.7B views, about $9.8M/yr estimated revenue ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/)). **[UNCONFIRMED]**: the exact numbers come from secondary sources.

**Scale of AI slop revenue: the Kapwing study, reported by The Guardian in late Dec 2025.**
- Of the top 100 trending channels in each country (about 15,000 channels), 278 were AI-slop-only.
- Those 278 had 221M subscribers and 63B views, with **about $117M/yr estimated ad revenue**.
- 104 of the first 500 videos shown to a fresh account (about 21%) were AI slop.
- Sources: [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/320275/Over-21-percent-videos-shown-to-new-YouTube-users-are-AI-slop-study-finds); [UkraNews](https://ukranews.com/en/amp/news/1125233-algorithms-promote-ai-garbage-low-quality-content-flooded-youtube-and-brings-in-hundreds-of-millions).
- Revenue is Kapwing's *estimate*, not reported earnings. This is the strongest evidence that AI channels *did* earn real money, concentrated in top-trending outliers, and it is the cohort YouTube then targeted.

**Individual cases. [UNCONFIRMED], third-party reporting.**
- A Bible-story channel (588k subscribers, reportedly about $30k/month) was fully demonetized for "inauthentic and mass-produced content".
- An exam-prep channel earning $7,500/month was hit by the same enforcement.
- One operator with 12 faceless channels had 7 demonetized.
- Sources: [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/).
- In June 2026 YouTube was reportedly testing viewer surveys to flag AI slop, with collateral damage to faceless human creators ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)).

**TikTok Creator Rewards.**
- TikTok's own Creator Academy states that unoriginal content "cannot earn" through Creator Rewards, and originality is a factor in the payout formula ([TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy)). The page's snippet does not mention AI.

#### SEO and affiliate sites
**Google enforcement history.**
- On 5 Mar 2024 Google launched its core update and new spam policies, including scaled content abuse. Manual actions followed on 6–7 Mar.
- Ian Nuttall's sample of 49,345 sites found **837 (1.7%) fully deindexed**.
- Originality.ai found 1,446 of 79k sites (2%) with manual actions. All had some AI posts and half were 90–100% AI. Originality.ai sells AI detection.
- Sources: [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-march-2024-core-update-impact-hundreds-of-websites-deindexed/510981/); [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/blog/google-march-2024-manual-action-ai-update-list).
- Scaled content abuse applies "regardless of how content is produced" ([bulkbase summary](https://bulkbase.ai/seo/scaled-content-abuse-googles-policy-enforcement-how-to-stay-compliant-in-2026)). Google's spam-policy page was blocked.

**Site reputation abuse ("parasite SEO").**
- Enforced from 7 May 2024 via manual actions only, usually on a subdirectory or subdomain ([CapConvert](https://www.capconvert.com/learn/blog/google-site-reputation-abuse-18-months)).
- From 30 Aug 2026, these manual actions **no longer apply in the EEA** after discussions with the European Commission, but still apply elsewhere ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-updates-site-reputation-abuse-policy-removes-penalties-in-eea/587423/)).

**2026 updates.**
- After the March 2026 core update, thin, affiliate-comparison and templated AI content reportedly lost 30–50% of visibility ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/march-2026-core-update-content-quality-winners-losers)). This is agency analysis.
- Glenn Gabe's (GSQi) case studies of the **August 2026 spam update** include scaled-content-abuse and AI-generated-content sites, one losing rankings for more than 200k queries ([GSQi](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)).
- Lily Ray analyzed 220+ sites showcased as AI-content "success stories": **54% lost ≥30% of peak organic traffic and 22% lost ≥75%** ([Cicero Studio summary](https://cicero.studio/en/blog/ai-content-scale-220-sites-study-seo-risks-2026/); [Lily Ray Substack](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/it-works-until-it-doesnt-ai-content-risks)). Seen via secondary summary.

**AI Overviews.**
- **Pew Research (Jul 2025; ~900 US adults, 68,879 searches, Mar 2025 data):** organic click rate was about 8% with an AI Overview vs about 15% without. Only 1% of visits clicked a source cited in the AI Overview. Google disputed the methodology ([SISTRIX](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/pew-research-ai-overviews-halve-click-through-rates/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-disputes-pew-study-showing-ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-by-half/)).
- **Ahrefs:** the top-ranking page's CTR is 34.5% lower when an AI Overview is present (Apr 2025). A re-run comparing Dec 2023 with Dec 2025 found **58% lower** (Feb 2026). This is correlational ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/); [MediaNama, Feb 2026](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-google-ai-overviews-click-through-rates-58-study/)).

**Case results.**
- HouseFresh, a hands-on review site, fell from about 4,000 to about 200 daily Google visitors over 18 months. That case is not AI-related, so even legitimate affiliates are suffering ([getchatads summary](https://www.getchatads.com/blog/is-affiliate-blogging-dead-what-the-data-actually-says/)).
- Make-money niche content reportedly lost 93% of traffic; a separate 100-blog study found a median 85% loss from 2022 to 2026 ([getchatads](https://www.getchatads.com/blog/affiliate-blogging-statistics/)). Secondary.

- No 2025–2026 revision was found. Medium's help page was blocked.

**Quora Partner Program.** Retired: English on 30 Sept 2022 and all languages on 24 Mar 2023, with the last payout on 3 Apr 2023 ([Quora Help Center](https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000673263-Partner-Program-Frequently-Asked-Questions); [TechCrunch, Aug 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/08/18/quora-shutting-down-english-version-partner-program/)).

**Vocal Media.**
- AI content is allowed but must be tagged. Unlabeled AI is "deceitful" and may lead to rejection, suspension or termination.
- Challenge entries detected as AI are disqualified.
- Sources: [Vocal Community Guidelines](https://vocal.media/resources/community-guidelines); [Vocal AI update](https://vocal.media/resources/an-update-from-vocal-on-ai-generated-content).
- Critics say Vocal's "Latest Stories" is full of AI spam ([Medium opinion](https://medium.com/@addisonalder/vocal-media-has-a-huge-ai-problem-6d41af30390b)).
- Per-read pay rates were not found.

#### AI music on streaming
- **Michael Smith (SDNY, 24 Cr. 504).**
  - He pleaded guilty in March 2026 to conspiracy to commit wire fraud.
  - **Sentenced 6 Oct 2026 to 18 months** in prison, 2 years of supervised release and **forfeiture of $8,091,843.64**.
  - The scheme ran 2017–2024: hundreds of thousands of AI-generated songs streamed billions of times by up to about 10,000 bot accounts on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music and YouTube Music.
  - Prosecutors had sought ≥46 months; Probation recommended 24.
  - **Judge Koeltl noted the prosecution targeted bot-faked streams, not the use of AI itself.**
  - Sources: [Music Ally, 7 Oct 2026](https://musically.com/2026/10/07/music-streaming-fraudster-michael-smith-gets-18-month-jail-sentence/); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/man-behind-8m-ai-song-and-bot-streaming-fraud-is-sentenced-to-18-months-in-prison/); [Bloomberg Law, plea](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/ai-music-maker-who-faked-streams-pleads-guilty-on-fraud-count). **[VERIFIED]**
- **US Copyright Office** launched a music-streaming fraud inquiry ([MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/music-streaming-fraud-inquiry-launched-by-us-copyright-office)). The date was not visible in the snippet.
- **Spotify, 25 Sept 2025:**
  - removed more than 75M "spammy" tracks in the prior 12 months;
  - a new spam filter (mass uploads, duplicates, SEO hacks, artificially short tracks) stops flagged tracks from being recommended;
  - an impersonation policy (voice clones need the artist's consent);
  - DDEX-based AI disclosures.
  - Sources: [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2025/digital/news/spotify-new-ai-safeguards-28228); [MBW](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-has-deleted-75m-spammy-tracks-as-it-unveils-new-ai-music-policies).
- AI Credits became visible in Spotify song credits on **16 Apr 2026**, delivered through distributors (DistroKid first) ([Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/spotify-ai-credits-live-disclosure-only-travels-through-your-distributor/)).
- Spotify's 1,000-streams-per-12-months royalty threshold (since 2024) means a long tail of low-play tracks earns $0 ([TechCrunch, Nov 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/21/spotify-upcoming-royalty-model-changes-generate-1b-artists-over-five-years)).
- **Deezer (primary newsroom):**
  - Apr 2026: AI tracks were about 44% of daily uploads, about 75k per day.
  - Jul 2026: more than 50% of new uploads in June.
  - Fully AI tracks get only 1–3% of streams.
  - **Up to 85% of streams on fully AI tracks in 2025 were fraudulent** and demonetized.
  - Deezer is taking down AI tracks used for fraud and those unstreamed for 6 months.
  - Sources: [Deezer, Apr 2026](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/04/ai-generated-tracks-represent-44-of-new-uploaded-music/); [Deezer, Jul 2026](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/); [TechCrunch, 20 Apr 2026](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/04/20/deezer-says-44-of-songs-uploaded-to-its-platform-daily-are-ai-generated/). **[VERIFIED: platform primary data]**

### Inferences
- **Adobe Stock arithmetic.** At Stock Performer's $0.17 per AI image per month (probably 2023 data, before about 50% of the library became AI), reaching $200/month needs about 1,200 accepted, non-similar AI images. With the "max 3 similar" rule and new upload limits, that takes months of accepted uploads. Per-image revenue has very likely fallen since. I rate ≥$200/month as **possible but unproven and slow**, with a low ToS risk if disclosed.
- **beehiiv Ad Network arithmetic.** At an illustrative $10 CPM, $200/month needs about 20,000 unique opens per month (for example, about 5,000 openers × 4 sends). Building that audience requires subscriber acquisition (paid ads or Boosts spending, cross-promotion, social). Acquisition is the hard, human-presence-heavy part. Every credible beehiiv success story involves a known human operator or brand.
- **YouTube.** The verified money in AI channels sat in a small set of trending outliers (278 of about 15,000 top channels). Those channels have been the explicit enforcement target since January 2026. A new, compliant, AI-heavy channel starting in October 2026 faces the YPP threshold plus "inauthentic content" review. The expected value of a mostly-automated channel is low and its variance high.
- **KDP.** The 2 titles per format per week cap (21 Sept 2026) structurally kills the "flood the catalog" model AI publishers relied on. Combined with mandatory disclosure and knock-off backlash, mass AI publishing is now both throttled and reputationally toxic. A small number of genuinely useful, disclosed, human-reviewed books remains allowed, but there is no evidence it reliably reaches $200/month.
- **Etsy and POD.** Policy still *allows* disclosed AI designs under "Designed by". But digital-download saturation, the January 2026 disclosure enforcement and Printify's 17 Sept 2026 "review, edit and approve" clause together imply a human checkpoint and a crowded market. Earnings evidence is entirely guru-sourced.
- No channel meets "≥$200/month, mostly automated, verifiable evidence, compliant" on the evidence found. Gaps of this kind are themselves strong signal, given how loudly gurus market these channels.

### Gaps
- KDP's own help pages (G200672390 content guidelines and the title-limit page), YouTube's YPP policy page, Google's spam-policies page, Etsy's Creativity Standards and Medium's AI policy were all blocked. Policy wording is secondhand.
- I found no independent dataset of earnings for AI-assisted KDP publishers, Etsy or POD AI shops, Merch by Amazon, Redbubble, Vocal or Medium in 2025–2026. The r/KDP, r/EtsySellers, r/NewTubers and r/juststart threads did not surface in search results.
- I have no current (2025–2026) per-image Adobe Stock AI revenue figure. The Stock Performer data is undated, likely 2023. The actual numbers in Adobe's new upload limits were not visible.
- I have no beehiiv ad network CPM distribution (median CPMs) and no verified AI-written newsletter revenue.
- Time to first revenue by channel was not found in sources. From general knowledge, *not verified this session*:
  - YPP requires 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
  - TikTok Creator Rewards requires 10k followers and 100k views in 30 days.
  - KDP pays royalties about 60 days after month end.
  - Adobe pays out at $25 minimum.
  - All of these should be confirmed against platform pages.
- I could not determine whether TikTok's Creator Rewards terms mention AI explicitly; third-party sources conflict.

## Key Question 2: Which are now effectively closed by policy, saturated, or legally risky (fraud, undisclosed AI, fake engagement)?

### Takeaway
**Closed or illegal**
- Bot-streamed AI music is prosecuted federal wire fraud: 18 months in prison and $8.09M forfeiture, Oct 2026.
- Fully AI-written paywalled Medium posts are ineligible since May 2024.
- Quora's Partner Program no longer exists.
- Getty and Shutterstock (AI made off-platform) do not accept AI uploads.

**Throttled or high enforcement risk**
- Mass AI KDP publishing: 2 titles per format per week cap from 21 Sept 2026, plus disclosure.
- Templated or mass-produced AI YouTube: "inauthentic content", with January 2026 terminations.
- TikTok Creator Rewards for fully AI videos: originality flags.
- Scaled AI SEO sites: scaled content abuse manual actions and 2026 spam updates.

**Saturated**
- Etsy digital downloads and printables.
- Adobe Stock: about half of all images are AI.
- AI music uploads: more than 50% of Deezer's new uploads, but only 1–3% of streams.
- SEO in general, where AI Overviews cut top-result CTR by about 34–58% even for compliant sites.

### Cited Findings
- **Illegal (fraud).** Smith's AI-music-plus-bot-stream scheme was wire-fraud conspiracy, sentenced 6 Oct 2026. The judge stressed that the bot-generated fake streams, not the AI, were the crime ([Music Ally](https://musically.com/2026/10/07/music-streaming-fraudster-michael-smith-gets-18-month-jail-sentence/); [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/man-behind-8m-ai-song-and-bot-streaming-fraud-is-sentenced-to-18-months-in-prison/)).
- Deezer found up to 85% of streams on fully AI tracks in 2025 were fraudulent and excluded them from royalties ([Deezer](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/)).
- Spotify reportedly demonetizes and filters mass uploads, duplicates and "artificially short tracks", and bans unauthorized voice impersonation ([Variety, Sept 2025](https://au.variety.com/2025/digital/news/spotify-new-ai-safeguards-28228)).
- **Closed by policy.**
  - Medium AI-generated paywall ban, effective 1 May 2024 ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/technology/medium-bans-ai-generated-content-from-its-paid-partner-program/)).
  - Quora Partner Program retired 2022–2023 ([Quora Help](https://help.quora.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000673263-Partner-Program-Frequently-Asked-Questions)).
  - Getty AI-upload ban, 2022 ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2022/09/21/getty-images-ban-ai-generated-pictures-shutterstock-following-suit)).
  - Shutterstock bars AI made outside its platform ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/203436/ai-generated-stock-photos-shutterstock-700-million-images)).
- **Throttled.**
  - KDP cap of 2 per format per week from 21 Sept 2026 ([ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/podcast-amazon-cuts-kdp-uploads-to-two-titles-a-week/)).
  - YouTube inauthentic-content policy (15 Jul 2025) and the January 2026 AI-channel shutdowns ([Finviz/BI](https://finviz.com/news/294709/youtube-doesnt-allow-spam-scams-or-other-deceptive-practices-ceo-neal-mohan-says-as-platform-shuts-popular-ai-channels)).
  - Adobe Stock "max 3 similar" spam rule with termination risk ([Adobe FAQ](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/help/generative-ai-faq.html)).
- **Undisclosed AI is the most common enforcement trigger.**
  - Etsy: "no mention of AI anywhere" is reported as the top removal reason ([iscompliant](https://iscompliant.app/Blog/etsy-creativity-standards-pod-sellers-guide)).
  - Vocal: undisclosed AI is "deceitful" ([Vocal guidelines](https://vocal.media/resources/community-guidelines)).
  - Medium: undisclosed AI gets network-only distribution ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/303573/20240414/medium-ai-generated-content-new-partner-program-policy.htm)).
  - KDP: non-disclosure leads to suppression or removal (blog-reported) ([AI Policy Desk](https://www.aipolicydesk.com/blog/amazon-kdp-ai-generated-content-surge-enforcement-2026)).
- **Legal and IP risk.**
  - Impersonating real authors on KDP is a recurring press scandal ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/amazon-ai-book-knockoffs-1235450690/); [NBC, Oct 2026](https://nbcnews.com/video/how-ai-is-impersonating-authors-work-270953541767)).
  - Redbubble and POD enforcement centers on trademark and IP regardless of how designs are generated ([profitlab360](https://profitlab360.com/blog/can-you-sell-ai-art-on-redbubble)).
  - The New York law on AI-generated humans in ads took effect 9 Jun 2026 ([Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/47658708553617-Do-I-need-to-disclose-that-my-AI-mockups-show-AI-generated-models)).
- **Saturation.**
  - Adobe Stock about 50% AI ([CineD](https://www.cined.com/ai-flood-on-adobe-stock-nearly-half-of-all-images-now-ai-generated/)).
  - Deezer: more than 50% of uploads AI, 1–3% of streams ([Deezer](https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/07/ai-music-exceeds-50-percent-daily-uploads-deezer/)).
  - Etsy sellers report sales collapses ([Digital Trends, Sept 2026](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/etsy-sellers-are-quitting-as-ai-slop-floods-the-handmade-marketplace/)).
  - Pew: about 8% vs 15% organic click rate with and without AI Overviews ([SISTRIX](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/pew-research-ai-overviews-halve-click-through-rates/)).
  - Ahrefs: 58% lower top-result CTR ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks/)).

### Inferences
**Bright red lines for this agent:**
1. Any artificial engagement (bot streams, views or reads; buying followers) is fraud, and in music it is now criminally prosecuted.
2. Undisclosed AI anywhere disclosure is required: KDP, Etsy, Adobe Stock, Vocal, Spotify via distributor, TikTok and YouTube realistic-content labels.
3. Impersonating real authors or artists, voice cloning, trademarked designs.
4. Mass near-duplicate uploads, which every platform treats as spam: KDP cap, Adobe "max 3 similar", Spotify spam filter, YouTube inauthentic content, Google scaled content abuse.

The business model that made AI content lucrative in 2023–2024 was volume. It is now precisely what policies across all eight channels penalize. That makes "mostly automated" and "compliant" increasingly hard to satisfy at once.

AI music for streaming royalties is effectively closed for an honest agent:
- Legit AI tracks get 1–3% of streams.
- The 1,000-stream threshold zeroes out the long tail.
- Any shortcut to streams is the Smith crime.

### Gaps
- Etsy's official enforcement statistics: the Q1 2026 figure of about 12,000 removals is unconfirmed.
- YouTube's official count of AI channel terminations is not published in a primary source I could access.
- No primary Google statement was found on whether scaled-content-abuse policy now governs AI Overview citations (claimed May 2026, unconfirmed).
- No FTC or other regulator action specifically against undisclosed AI-generated books or POD listings was found.

## Key Question 3: Which require the owner's ongoing personal presence?

### Takeaway
Explicit "personal presence" rules are rarer than *de facto* ones.
- **YouTube** officially allows faceless and AI-assisted channels.
- **Medium's** paywall effectively requires a human author.
- **Printify** now contractually requires human review of AI output before publishing.
- Every channel with credible revenue stories (newsletters, YouTube, Etsy) relies on a human brand, human audience-building or human editorial judgment.

Channels closest to "set up once, then automated" are **Adobe Stock** (disclosed AI uploads within the similarity limits) and, to a lesser degree, **KDP** and **POD listings**. They are also the ones with the weakest earnings evidence and the most saturation.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube** says faceless channels remain eligible, and AI use alone is not the trigger. Inauthentic, mass-produced content is ([ytgrowth](https://ytgrowth.io/blog/youtube-ai-policy); [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)). A reported July 2026 clarification targets AI personas presenting as human experts in health, legal, finance or politics ([noobclaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-2026/)) **[UNCONFIRMED]**. That implies an AI "host" must not pretend to be a human expert.
- **Medium:** AI-generated writing is barred from the paywall, so earning requires human-written posts ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/technology/medium-bans-ai-generated-content-from-its-paid-partner-program/)).
- **Printify:** users must "review, edit, and approve AI-Generated Content before publishing it or submitting an Order" (17 Sept 2026 terms) ([Printify policy updates](https://printify.com/policies/updates/)).
- **TikTok:** the safest eligibility path described is the creator's own script, voice and edits. Fully AI-generated visuals plus voiceover are the most exposed ([Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/what-tiktoks-ai-monetization-restrictions-signal-for-creator-income/); [socialboostdigital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-unoriginal-content-flag)).
- **Newsletters:** beehiiv's showcased earners are human-led brands. Koerner's built on his personal brand and podcast ([beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/case-studies/koerner-office?via=tgs)); AI Central is a media brand with editorial staff implied ([beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/case-studies/ai-central)). Substack now offers on-request AI-detection scans and "How I make this" disclosures, which favors transparent human involvement ([NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/substack-launches-ai-detection-tools-with-pangram-to-flag-ai-generated-content/)).
- **Adobe Stock** requires only the AI checkbox, category placement and similarity limits for AI content. No personal-presence requirement was found ([Adobe GenAI guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html)).
- **KDP** requires disclosure and respect for upload limits. No personal-presence rule was found, but the title cap of 21 Sept 2026 limits throughput ([ALLi](https://selfpublishingadvice.org/podcast-amazon-cuts-kdp-uploads-to-two-titles-a-week/)).

### Inferences
**Ranking for an autonomous, compliance-first agent** (most to least automatable), with the realistic outlook for each:

1. **Adobe Stock.** Disclosed AI illustrations and vectors with strong keywording. The upside is low (likely tens of dollars per month for months), but the ToS path is clear.
2. **KDP.** A few genuinely useful, disclosed, human-quality reference or workbook titles. Weekly caps apply, and there is no evidence it reaches $200/month.
3. **Etsy and POD.** "Designed by" plus AI disclosure, subject to Printify's human-review clause and severe saturation. There are also identity and verification steps and customer-service obligations, which are general platform requirements not verified this session.
4. **Newsletters.** Content generation is automatable; audience growth and ad approval are the hard part. A transparent "AI-curated" label is advisable.
5. **YouTube and TikTok.** High enforcement variance against AI-heavy channels. The YPP and Creator Rewards thresholds make time to first revenue long.
6. **SEO and affiliate.** Scaled AI content is the explicit target of Google policy, and AI Overviews compress clicks even for compliant sites.
7. **Medium, Quora, Vocal.** Medium requires human writing; Quora's program no longer exists; Vocal pay rates are unknown and the platform has a spam problem.
8. **AI music streaming.** Only legitimate as a labeled hobby, with near-zero revenue. Any engagement manipulation is a crime.

Even "automated" channels need the owner for one-time identity, tax and payment setup, and for any appeal or verification request. Whether platforms allow an AI agent to *operate* the account (as opposed to generating content) was **not** researched here. Platform automation and bot clauses in seller ToS are a separate question the report writer should flag.

### Gaps
- I did not verify per-platform ToS clauses on automated account operation (API vs browser automation) for KDP, Etsy, Adobe Stock, beehiiv or YouTube.
- Identity and tax verification requirements, and whether periodic re-verification (for example, Etsy seller video verification) needs the owner live, were not researched.
- No first-hand 2026 report was found from someone running any of these channels with a genuinely autonomous agent and publishing verifiable revenue.
